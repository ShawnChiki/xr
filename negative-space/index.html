Negative

You are White, standing on the ground. Black lives upside down under it and digs up into your sky. Every tunnel they dig rises here as rock. Each game you play White or Black.

Space

Your ruby is buried somewhere under the ground. Melt down to it and touch it to win.

The floating ruby is Black's target. If one of their towers swallows it, you lose. Drag it away or melt the towers.

Orbs float in the sky and hide in the rock, and your rival can grab them too. Gold widens your beam and blue speeds it up for 15 seconds. Orange gives 12 grenades. The black bomb is one huge throw. The eye lets you see your rival through the rock, and the spiky violet trap is a mine you set in the air: when their tower grows into it, it blows the tower apart. Ringed compass orbs sit at the very top or bottom and point roughly toward your ruby.
