You are White, standing on the ground. Black lives upside down under it and digs up into your sky. Every tunnel they dig rises here as rock.

Space

Your ruby is buried somewhere under the ground. Melt down to it and touch it to win.

The floating ruby is Black's target. If one of their towers swallows it, you lose. Drag it away or melt the towers.

Orbs float in the sky and hide in the rock, and Black can grab them too. Gold widens your beam and blue speeds it up for 15 seconds. Orange gives 6 grenades. Ringed compass orbs sit at the very top or bottom and point roughly toward your ruby.
