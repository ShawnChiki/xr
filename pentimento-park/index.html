Headset controls
Trigger
Use the tool at the pen tip: draw, bend, flatten, smooth, mark, erase bits or erase whole lines. Point at the menu and pull to press a button.
Ride tool
A fingerboard hangs under your controller (your Party Lines fit, if you set one). Hold A and ride it to record a run · trigger: random flip · B: refit the board (B again locks it) · stick press: back to Draw
Holding a run
Move it; swing your hand to turn it · A lays ties under it · B removes them · stick press deletes the run
Grip
Near a point on a strict line: grab and move it. Near a strict line between points: adds a point there. Anywhere else: move and turn the model; both grips scale it.
Holding a point
A / X deletes it · B / Y makes it a sharp corner (or smooth again)
Stick ← →
Line type: Loose, Guide, Crease, Strict crease, Edge, Strict edge
Stick ↑ ↓
Brush size for bending, marking and erasing
Hold A
Tool dial: push the stick (or move your hand) toward a tool, let go of A to pick it
Hold B
In Sketch: line type dial (Loose, Guide, Crease, Strict crease, Edge, Strict edge). Elsewhere: step dial: jump to 1 Ride, 2 Sketch, 3 Surface, 4 SubD, 5 Fab or 6 Files, or Tips, Room and Settings. Holding the other hand's trigger opens it too
Steps
The step you're on decides what your buttons do: Ride records runs, Sketch draws, SubD edits the control points
Grip the menu
With your main hand: leave it floating in the room. Bring it back to your other hand to put it back
X / Y
Undo / redo
Right stick click
Jump between Draw and Erase bits
Room button
Top of the menu: switch between seeing your room (MR) and full VR, any time
Left stick click
Hide or show the menu on your left hand
