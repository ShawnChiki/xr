Your belt

Tools float at your waist. Reach down and squeeze the grip to take one. Let go and it goes back, except the hook, which you throw.

Quest

  • Grip on an edge, bar, hold or rope: hang on. Pull to climb, let go mid-pull to fling
  • A / X: jump · B / Y: the little world, to teleport
  • Left stick: walk · Right stick: turn
  • Holding a rope, pull the trigger near a form or bar to tie it there. Hold two rope ends together and pull a trigger to knot them

Computer

  • WASD walk · Space jump · F fly (Space / C up and down)
  • 1–7 take a tool, 0 empty hand · Left click use it
  • Right click with an empty hand: hold on, then look around to pull
  • M: little world · Esc: menu

About

The forms start as AI images turned into 3D: half-right playgrounds, spontaneous and unpredictable. Nobody designed them to be climbed.

Players give them use by moving over them. Everyone shares a small supply of steel and rope that refills slowly. Hooks stay where they catch, ropes stay where they're tied, so the next climber finds a route already started. Cut a rope and it stays cut until someone ties a knot.

Raw forms only hold you at real edges: a lip, a thin bar, the rim of a hole. A hook only catches on an edge too. Carving a pocket into a wall makes a new one.

Then the field goes wild again, and the next group starts over.
