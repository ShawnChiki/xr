World

Add your own models. Put the height in the file name, like tower_10m.glb. With no number they come in 4 m tall. Colours come from the texture or vertex colours. Models that overlap fuse into one.

    Tools

    Quest

    • Grip: hold on. Pull yourself up The Climb style, let go mid-pull to fling
    • Trigger: use that hand's tool. Hold on with one hand, draw with the other
    • A / X: next tool for that hand
    • B / Y: the little model of the world. Point and pull the trigger to go there
    • Left stick: walk · Right stick: turn
    • Holding a rope? Pull that hand's trigger to reel yourself up

    Computer

    • WASD walk · Space jump · F fly (Space / C up and down)
    • Left click: use tool · Right click: hold on, then look around to pull
    • 1–7 or scroll: pick a tool · M: model of the world · Esc: menu

    About

    The forms start as AI images turned into 3D: half-right playgrounds, spontaneous and unpredictable. Nobody designed them to be climbed.

    Players give them use by moving over them. Everyone shares a small supply of steel, holds and rope that refills slowly. To build somewhere new you take back something you built before. Every grapple line stays where it lands, so the next climber finds a route already started.

    Raw surfaces only hold you at real edges: a lip, a thin bar, the rim of a hole. Carving a pocket into a wall makes a new edge.

    Then the field goes wild again, and the next group starts over.
