ChikiBallzVR

Hide & seek inside a solid cube carved into rooms and tunnels at every angle. Seekers wear a gold ring, and a touch turns you into one.

Settings

In the headset, press the menu button on your left controller (or hold Y) to pause and open this menu.

Controls

Left stick rolls your ball the way the view faces · right stick looks around (push left to look left, up to look up) · A/X hop · B pop.

Trigger: hold and point to aim a FALL, let go to fall that way. The FALL and POP rings in the corner of your view fill up as they recharge.

Tabletop: grip grabs the model, two hands to scale it.

Desktop: WASD roll · Q/E turn · Space hop · click a direction to FALL · F pop
