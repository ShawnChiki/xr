Bot toddler

1. Set the floor: the grid is the game's floor. Press Confirm, or pinch / pull the trigger to move it to your hand: raise it to a table or chest height to play standing up. It remembers your choice.
2. Outline your space: point at the floor and hold the trigger (or pinch) to draw round your clear space, like the Quest's own boundary. Undo or Clear if you slip, then press Done. The bedroom (pink) is the biggest room with straight walls that fits inside your line.
3. Be the toddler: play on your knees and really crawl. No sticks needed; your hands are giant baby hands.
Grab: make a fist or pinch near a toy (or squeeze grip on a controller). Throw it at the toy box: anything that lands close gets pulled in.
Mouth it: hold a toy up to your mouth and it gets drooly and slow. Snacks get eaten (one less thing to put away, and it wakes you up). Tantrum: slap the floor with both hands at once (or pull both triggers).
The toys fight dirty: they knock over block towers, books and snacks, spill the crayons, put on a lullaby (your eyes start closing: slap the record player to stop it) and switch off the lamp (touch the lamp to turn it back on).
Win: get the room tidier than it started (the toy box shows the mess) before the wall clock says NAP, or put everything away for an instant win. Your giant hands knock things over too, so be careful. Mom pops her head in at the start, with two minutes and one minute to go, and opens the door at nap time…

You're a toy

📱 Full screen on iPhone?

Waiting for the toddler to start. Hold your phone either way.

Move: left thumb, like steering: left and right turn you, up goes forward. Turn and look: drag anywhere else. The camera swings round behind you.
Jump and climb: run at bars, nets and walls. You can climb the toddler too.
Your job: make a mess. If the room is messier than at the start when nap time comes, the toys win. Every thing you knock over pops up as +1 mess.
Grab = Shove. Run at a block tower and shove it over. Climb the shelves and shove the books off. Jump onto the snack table and shove the food and the crayon cups onto the floor (or jump up under the table and everything on it bounces off). Shove a giant hand and it lets go.
Pranks: press Grab at the record player for a lullaby: the toddler's eyes start closing. Press Grab on the lamp's floor switch: lights out, the toddler can barely see. Next to the toy box, Grab flings things back out. Holding a loose part, Grab throws it.
Special: every toy has one move (it tells you when you become it): wreckers 💥 knock stuff over, pranksters 😜 mess with the toddler, movers 🪽 get up high. Swap jumps into the nearest free toy. If you get put away you wake up in another toy. When mom looks in, freeze like a real toy!

Full screen on iPhone

Safari on iPhone can't hide its bars for a web page, but a Home Screen app can, and it opens with no Safari bars at all:
  1. Tap the ⬆ Share button in Safari (bottom bar, or ••• then Share).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Toy Box from your Home Screen. It goes straight to the join page.
