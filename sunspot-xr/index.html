SUNSPOT XR

Record up to a minute of voice. Every chop becomes a sun in a 3D cloud in your room, with similar sounds floating together. Pinch inside the cloud to arpeggiate the suns near your fingers, or draw a path through them and reshape it with its handles. Works with just your hands (controllers too).

On a computer: hold the left mouse button near suns to play · right-drag to look around · scroll to zoom. In the Quest browser, tap REC once here first so the mic is allowed before you go into XR.
