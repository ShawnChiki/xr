SUBDECK

Sculpt a park in SubD with your controllers, then shrink to fingerboard size and skate it. Your hands are your legs.

Sculpt controls
Trigger
Use the tool. Shapes (Surface, Block, Blob, Tube, Bowl) are dragged out corner to corner
Edit tool
Trigger a point, edge or face and drag it. A quick tap selects it; tap more to move them together. Tap empty air to clear
While dragging
A / X extrudes from where you are (press again for more segments). B / Y makes a held edge sharp
Grip
Grab a whole object, rail or spawn. Grip it with both hands to scale it. Grip empty air to move the whole park; both grips scale it
Grip + A / X
Leave a copy behind. Grip + B / Y deletes it
Mirror
On the panel: everything you make and change repeats across the pink plane

Skate controls
Hands
They are your feet on the board. Turn the line between them to steer, raise them to crouch
Trigger
Booster. It pushes toward whichever end of the board you look at, so look back to go fakie
Ollie
The board sits flat until you clearly tip your feet line. Lower your back hand well below the front and the nose lifts (a manual). Snap it down fast: the tail smacks the ground and pops you up (harder snap, higher pop). Lift your front hand to level the board, or land steep and bail. Same with the front hand for a nollie. A also ollies. Hold the tail down to drag-brake. Roll your hands in the air to flip
