Fingers

Finger and tab sizes are full size and scale with the model. Gaps and allowances are as cut. Wide-angle joints get extra finger length so they sand flush.

Ribs & tabs

Ribs are spaced evenly, never further apart than the max, and nudge away from awkward inside corners. Slots always keep at least the minimum wood to every panel edge.

Ribs go in longest first, swapping direction each time. Each rib's open slots face up for the next one, so the waffle stands on its own while you build; R1 goes in first. Every rib stays one piece: where a cut-out would split one, it shifts over, or is left out and the cut-out's lining panels brace that stretch.

Corner blocks

Joints further off square than the screw angle limit get small angled blocks inside. Each one tabs into both walls and takes a screw on either side of each tab, four per block, so the walls pull flush.

Screws & labels

Pilot holes along the joints, checked for clashes, and engraved part names.

Pilot holes

Joints further off square than the angle limit get no screws, since a screw would come out the side of the mating panel.

Labels

The center reads box + panel, like A3. Each edge reads panel + edge > mating panel + edge, like 3B>5D. Labels move or shrink to stay clear of slots, ribs, holes and each other.

Machine

Bits, depths and dog-ears. Panels and sheets show what will come off the machine.

Cutting

Toolpath corners

Dog-ears follow your script: the bit-center path spikes along the corner bisector just far enough for the bit to reach the true corner. Chamfer trims the long miter tips on outside corners; 0 leaves them.

Engraving

Laser

Cut lines are offset by half the kerf so parts come out true to size.

Sharp bits

Every outline shrinks by this much, then grows back. Wedge tips and slivers thinner than twice it disappear before they get cut; everything else comes back exactly as drawn. 0 turns it off.

    Jumper

    Hard parkour landings run about 3–5× body weight; a hard tic-tac push about 1×. Grip: 0.5 for plywood on concrete, 0.3 on smooth floors, 0.7 on rubber. Strength counts only the screws, corner blocks and tabs near each load: a #8 screw holds about 90 lb sideways and 60 lb of pull-out, a corner block screw about 70 lb, glue about 100 psi, and plywood is allowed 2,500 psi in bending (all with a safety margin).

    How it works

    A planar grammar: boxes and cut planes in, labeled flat-pack plywood out.

    1. Mass it. Add boxes, then press P and drag any face to push or pull it, cut faces included. W move, E rotate, R size with the gumball. Snap keeps pushes to whole inches.
    2. Slice it. Add cut planes to a box and angle them. Each plane becomes a new panel.
    3. Panelize. Switch to Panels. Faces get finger joints, ribs tab up through slots, and labels are cut in with the engraving bit's shape.
    4. Read the labels. A3 is box A, panel 3. Along each edge, 3B>5D means panel 3's edge B meets panel 5's edge D.
    5. Carve it. Add cut-out to carve a box-shaped bite out of a box: a step, a notch, a tunnel or a window. The obstacle stays one continuous shell: holes go right through, inside corners get their own fingers, and ribs follow the new shape.
    6. Undo anything with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z. Save and open designs as files from the Model tab.
    7. Headset. On a Quest, tap VR or XR. Trigger a face to push or pull it, grip to grab and move. The menu on your left wrist adds boxes, cuts and cut-outs, deletes, undoes and switches views.
    8. Build it. The Build view plays the assembly step by step, checking that every part can slide in without its wood passing through another. It flags parts that can't go in and parts that overlap.
    9. Test it. The Test view sets each obstacle down on any face it can rest on, then shows where a shove tips or slides it, where a landing flips it, and how much the top panels flex. Hit it to watch.
    10. Cut. Pick the scale and stock, set the machine, then save the sheets as DXF.

    Each settings group has a diagram: hover a field to light its dimension, or click a dimension to jump to its field. Del removes the selection, Esc clears it. Your model saves in this browser.
