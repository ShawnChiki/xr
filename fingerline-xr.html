Headset controls
Trigger
Draw with the tool you picked: freehand, curve points, sharp points, edit points, eraser or start flag. Point at the menu and pull to press a button.
Grip
Near a ramp, rail or the start flag: pick it up and move it. In empty space: move the whole park. Both grips: turn and scale it. Riders keep riding.
Left hand
Holds the menu, flat like a tray. Click the left stick to hide or show it.
