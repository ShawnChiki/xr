Parkour games in an endless candy megacity

Settings

Match settings apply from the next round.

How to play

Left thumb
Drag anywhere on the left to run
Right side
Drag to look around
Buttons
JUMP, DASH and SLIDE. PASS pops up when you carry a case.
Move
WASD to run. Click the game to look with the mouse (Esc frees it). ← → turn the camera, the wheel zooms.
Keys
Space jump · Shift or left-click dash · C or right-click slide · E pass · H hides the key list
Gamepad
Sticks run and look · A jump · X or RT dash · B or LT slide · Y or bumpers pass · Start pause
Auto-parkour
Just steer. You vault (speed, lazy, kong, double kong, dash, reverse, thief, gate and cartwheel vaults; pick a favourite in Settings), climb walls, leap gaps (sometimes in a stride or a superman dive), swing round pipe columns, jump for pipes overhead, spin off the end of a wall run, tic-tac off walls you run at on a slant, throw a flip when you run off a big drop, stick hero landings, slide under low gaps, surf down steep roofs and pull up ledges by yourself.
Bars
Run or jump at a bar to grab it and swing. Keep steering to swing across monkey bars; Jump lets go with a big launch, Slide drops off.
Lines & pipes
Powerlines are ziplines: jump up, grab one and ride it downhill. Run along a pipe to grab it and shimmy hand over hand, or balance-run along the top.
Swing up
Under a walkway or ledge? Press Jump to swing up on top.
Air tricks
Jump again in the air to flip: push forward for a front flip, back for a backflip, sideways for a side flip. Fast flips can turn into corkscrews, doubles and butterfly twists, and long leaps into websters and gainers (Settings lets you pick your flip style or hide move names). Next to a wall it's a wall kick instead, up to three in a row. Jump right after you grab a wall for a wall flip.
Slide
Keeps your speed and ducks under things. Jump out of it for a long jump. Slide in the air to dive, then roll out. Slide while going slow to cartwheel, or standing still to windmill.
Rolls
Big drops at speed turn into a roll so you keep running. Chain tricks quickly for a combo.
Landmarks
Giant spiral towers, rainbows, planets and donut stacks rise over the city. Run up them, then slide back down.
Dash
Once per jump (Shift). Chasing? Dash near your target and you lunge at them, even if they're beside or behind you. Being chased? Your dash steers itself away from the chasers.
Split screen
Turn on Players: 2 on the title screen (or in Settings). With one controller, P1 plays on keyboard and mouse and P2 on the controller. With two controllers you each get one. No controller? P2 can use the arrow keys · Enter jump · Right Shift dash · Right Ctrl or / slide · . juke.
Juke
About to get caught? Hit JUKE (F or E) to snap sideways with a spin. For a split second nobody can tag you. Recharges in a couple of seconds.
Tag
Whoever is IT runs faster and can lunge: dash near a runner to launch at them. Don't be IT when time runs out.
Shadow tag
Pick Shadow in Custom game. You score every second you're near IT, and up to three times as fast in their blind spot right behind them (the glowing wedge). Getting tagged costs 30% of your points, and IT scores nothing until they tag someone. Most points wins.
VIP
Opposite tag. Everyone hunts the VIP. Tag them (or lunge with dash) to steal the crown.
Score
You earn a point every second you're the VIP. Most points when time runs out wins.
Getaway
Everyone takes a turn as the VIP. On your turn you score faster the further you are from the nearest chaser (full points 20 m away). On everyone else's turn you score by staying right next to the VIP. Nobody gets tagged. Most points after everyone's turn wins.
Highways
No camping the elevated highways: up there the VIP is slower and scores nothing (you can switch this off in Settings).
Crown
A fresh VIP can't be tagged for a moment, and the VIP runs a tiny bit slower (change it in Settings).
