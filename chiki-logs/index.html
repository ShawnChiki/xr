    Log section

    Joints

    Each joint gets one color, spread at random through the jam, and no log carries the same color twice. A colored sleeve runs through the middle of both logs, so on site red meets red.

    Safety

    A rod needs a straight line on one side long enough to slide it in and 5″ of wrench room on the other. Openings are checked between pairs of logs and inside the frames logs make with each other and the ground.
